Frequently Asked · Before you apply
01Who can register as a vendor?

Any registered brand, manufacturer, artisan, or FPO based in India — with a special focus on authentic Bengali products. You'll need a valid PAN, GST (if applicable), and a bank account in the business name.

02What documents are required to onboard?

PAN card, cancelled cheque or bank passbook (first page), and — if you are GST-registered — your GST certificate. Food sellers should also share their FSSAI licence if they have one. Payout bank details are collected separately, only after your application is approved, via a private secure link.

03How much commission does Sonar Bangla Haat take?

0% on your first 30 sales as a Founding Vendor. Thereafter, a tiered 7%–9% commission depending on your category — no flat cut, no hidden listing fees, no surprise deductions.

04How long does approval take?

Our team reviews every application within 24 hours. Once approved, your branded storefront goes live within 48–72 hours and we personally guide you through your first product listing.

05When and how do I receive payouts?

Payouts settle on a regular weekly cycle directly to your registered bank account, with a transparent statement of every sale, fee, and adjustment. Bank details are collected via a private, encrypted link only after approval.
